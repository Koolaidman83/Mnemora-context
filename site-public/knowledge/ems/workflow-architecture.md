# EMS knowledge and workflow architecture

## Workflow
Dispatch and context -> scene safety -> primary assessment -> clinical decision support -> intervention documentation -> transport/handoff -> case review -> quality improvement -> educational feedback.

## Knowledge capture
Capture timestamps, objective findings, decisions, rationale, protocol version, handoff, and follow-up when authorized. Keep identifiable run reports in private approved systems. Public case studies must be synthetic or reviewed for de-identification.

## Engineering goals
Offline-first field notes, source-linked protocol retrieval, structured handoff templates, physician-directed case review and audit-friendly change history.

## Safety
EMS protocols vary by jurisdiction and medical director. No public AI output replaces local protocols, medical control or clinician judgment.
