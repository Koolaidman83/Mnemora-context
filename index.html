MNEMORA · LOCAL-FIRST AI MEMORY

Make memory useful.

Mnemora is a working, local-first personal knowledge and AI memory system that organizes conversations, documents, study materials, and project decisions so useful context can be retrieved across tools and devices.

Project bio

Built around a private memory hub, searchable source index, portable device workers, and AI integration adapters. Its priorities are preserving original information, making context reusable, and keeping the owner in control of private data.

Status: Operational system, under active development. The SQLite memory service, local worker infrastructure, and private Tailscale connectivity have run on real hardware. Broader cross-AI integration and automated callbacks remain works in progress.

About the Founder

Mike is a career paramedic with more than three decades of emergency medical services experience, a nursing student, and an independent engineer and technology developer.

His work spans advanced life support and prehospital emergency care, clinical education, Linux and Android systems, distributed computing, hardware restoration, and local artificial intelligence. He has built and operated practical systems on repurposed computers and phones, with a particular interest in making older equipment useful again.

While balancing emergency medical work with nursing school, Mike built Mnemora to connect knowledge scattered across conversations, lecture recordings, textbooks, technical projects, and devices. Mnemora has operated as a real network of services and workers, including a SQLite-backed memory API, local processing, and private device connectivity. Its ongoing development focuses on reliability, safe workload distribution, and carrying useful context between AI tools. His interests extend to critical care, medical education, communications, sensing, and resilient personal computing.
