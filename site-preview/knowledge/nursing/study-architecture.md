# Nursing study architecture

Status: public design documentation; not a claim of fully deployed features.

## Learning pipeline
1. Ingest only authorized textbooks, lectures, personal notes, tables and figures.
2. Preserve source identifiers, chapter/page, timestamps, edition and original files.
3. Map concepts to an ontology: physiology, assessment, findings, differential, interventions, contraindications, monitoring, complications and escalation.
4. Generate source-grounded clinical scenarios and explanations, separating course claims from verified clinical evidence.
5. Assess single-best-answer, select-all-that-apply, prioritization, ordered-response and calculations.
6. Track errors by concept, cognitive step and unsafe distractor; schedule remediation.
7. Support accessible audio and low-resource mobile study.

## Guardrails
Public documents contain frameworks and synthetic examples, not proprietary course content, patient data, instructor recordings or student records. This material is educational, not patient-specific clinical direction.
