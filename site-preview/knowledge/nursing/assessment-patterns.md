# Assessment-pattern modeling

## Generalized scenario-to-question method
- Identify an important clinical decision embedded in a lecture narrative or case.
- Extract trigger findings, time pressure, competing priorities and safety constraints.
- Test recognition, interpretation, prioritization, action and reassessment separately.
- Construct plausible distractors based on sequencing errors, omitted assessments, contraindications or premature interventions.
- Require a rationale for every choice and identify which source supports it.
- Increase difficulty by adding evolving vital signs, conflicting data, dosage calculations or multiple simultaneous risks.

## Formats
Single best answer; SATA; ordered response; calculations; multi-stage cases; rapid prioritization.

## Attribution
This is a generalized assessment-design framework, not an assertion about any named instructor's confidential test bank or intentions. Never publish real exam items or private lecture recordings without authorization.
