# The Glovertucky Chronicles

An original fictionalized narrative.

Characters, geography, institutions, chronology and
incidents are altered or composite. No real patient,
personnel member or private establishment is identified.

## The Banana Ship

An aerospace-trained paramedic joins a rural ambulance
service. Its flagship is an aging diesel ambulance with
mismatched paint and improvised repairs.

The station seems to belong to another century.
Unreliable equipment and communications challenge the
crew, and the protagonist works to improve both.

## Blank Check

An early assignment involves a difficult critical-care
transport during a winter storm. Equipment fails,
testing the crew's resourcefulness and judgment.

The fictional patient survives. The protagonist
subsequently assumes greater clinical responsibility.

## The Other Glovertucky

Secluded wealth and rural hardship coexist in the
mountains. Patients come from every walk of life.

The protagonist learns that appearances reveal little
about the person behind a door. Equal care and
confidentiality are not for sale.

## Ice Mountain

Near the end of the agency's existence, the crew
confronts a respiratory emergency during an icy
mountain journey.

Another crew needs assistance with a separate
emergency. Both fictional patients survive.

The agency eventually closes.

## The Last Survivor

The Banana Ship outlives its agency. The protagonist
dreams of preserving the old ambulance.

An unreliable location record inspires a running joke
about experimental rocket propulsion.

The rocket propulsion is humor, not literal canon.
